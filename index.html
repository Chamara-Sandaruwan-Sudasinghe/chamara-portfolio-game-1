Quick guide

How to play

Use W / ↑ to accelerate, S / ↓ to slow down or reverse, and A / ← / D / → to steer. On mobile, use the on-screen arrows. Drive close to a glowing checkpoint and slow down to unlock its details. You can also choose a stop in the Journey Map to preview its story without driving there.

This is a local, self-contained demo. PDF links work when the documents folder stays next to index.html. Sound is optional and only starts after you turn it on.
